# Pulse Runner agent instructions

Pulse Runner is a compact Godot game and a GameFactory fixture. Preserve its core loop: move, collect pulses, avoid drones, and receive immediate readable feedback.

- `tuning.json` is the campaign's normal creative surface. Do not modify other files unless a campaign explicitly grants them.
- The seeded Godot scenario is a repeatable comparison tool, not proof that a game is fun.
- Favor changes a player can perceive within a short run. Consider control feel, readability, risk, pacing, recovery, and replayability together.
- Treat the neon style profile and design intent as constraints when they are supplied, while retaining freedom over solutions not fixed by them.
- Report assumptions and preserve evidence for critics. Do not claim a human playtest occurred unless an actual human record is supplied.
