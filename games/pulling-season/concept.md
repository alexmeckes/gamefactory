## Selected co-created concept

Pulling Season is a first-person physics gardening comedy about raising oversized, temperamental produce and deciding when and how to harvest it. The player is an underqualified gardener who reads visible crop and soil cues, chooses between safer early value and riskier late value, then physically wrestles crops out of the ground with movement, leverage, ropes, and simple tools. Harvested produce remains a world object whose damage and handling affect the result. The first proof is single-player and uses one small field, one crop, and one complete harvest decision before any networking, broad progression, content catalog, or final visual production.
