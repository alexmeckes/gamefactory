- Preserve the central tradeoff: roots carry both hydration and blight.

- Do not claim that deterministic scenarios prove fun or human comprehension.

- Keep scenario hooks honest and on the same simulation path as interactive play.
- Prefer one readable causal chain over additional resources, traits, currencies, or unit types.
- Keep external credentials and model-provider output out of prompts, traces, and committed project files.
