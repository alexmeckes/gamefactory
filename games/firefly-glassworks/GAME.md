# Firefly Glassworks

## Hook

Rotate a small set of physical glass instruments to guide one living firefly
beam through a miniature workshop. The same beam language should deepen from a
single reflection into splitting, color, heat, timing, and mechanisms without
turning into a generic pipe-rotation puzzle.

## Product target

- A polished mouse-first Godot puzzle game lasting roughly 8–12 minutes.
- One immediately understandable verb, a compact sequence of authored puzzles,
  and a meaningful final recombination of learned rules.
- Direct cause and effect: the beam travels, strikes, changes, and arrives. The
  player should not need to read a dashboard to understand the result.
- Reset and replay are always available. Failure should invite another idea,
  not punish experimentation.

## Exploration space

The exact instruments, puzzle topology, number of levels, progression, final
mechanical twist, scoring, and interaction details are open. Candidates should
state a hypothesis and make a bounded choice. Preserve the identity of one
living beam in a glass workshop; avoid accumulating familiar puzzle mechanics
without a unifying consequence.

## Evidence boundary

Deterministic scenarios can establish solvability, stability, input coverage,
progression, reset, and whether authored rules are exercised. Model critics can
identify likely clarity or degeneracy problems. Neither establishes that the
game is fun; human play remains the authority for that claim.
