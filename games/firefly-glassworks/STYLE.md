# Firefly Glassworks direction

Begin with quiet, precise glasswork rather than spectacle: smoke-dark benches,
aged brass fixtures, imperfect hand-blown glass, tiny living amber light, and
cool moonlit receivers. The beam is always the clearest moving subject.

This file is direction, not an asset order. Before the gameplay gate, agents
may use only a functional graybox. After gameplay selection, one representative
puzzle must prove an engine-feasible production method at the shipping camera.
Only that accepted vocabulary may be expanded.

Avoid glossy mobile cards, neon cyber grids, generic fantasy laboratories,
overgrown HUDs, floating explanatory prose, particle fog, and concept-art
detail that cannot survive normal gameplay scale. Important state must remain
distinguishable without relying on hue alone.
