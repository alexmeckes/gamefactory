# Firefly Glassworks agent instructions

- Prove the interaction before building production assets.
- Preserve `factory_setup`, `factory_tick`, `factory_sample`, and
  `factory_collect`; metrics must derive from simulated state.
- Do not present deterministic or model evidence as human evidence of fun.
- Treat concept and material studies as inspiration only. A production target
  must be captured from the running Godot project at the shipping camera.
- Keep phase boundaries honest: gameplay candidates do not make art; art-slice
  candidates finish one representative puzzle; production candidates expand
  only the accepted vocabulary.
- Preserve room to explore. Explain the hypothesis, then make a bounded choice
  rather than mechanically implementing every suggestion.
