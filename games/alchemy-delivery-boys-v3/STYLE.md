# Alchemy Delivery Boys — visual production constraints

Choose the final direction from reviewed whole-screen gameplay targets derived from the accepted live encounter.

- Build authored, expressive pixel art with strong silhouettes and a warm, peculiar alchemy-garden identity.
- Keep one declared pixel grid and a consistent world/character scale across every target and extracted asset.
- Use pixel art for world and character rendering; use readable typography appropriate to each information role instead of forcing every sentence into a tiny pixel font.
- The viewport must read as a place characters inhabit, not a dashboard wrapped around an illustration.
- Potion state, route transformation, interactable props, destination, villager reaction, and next-choice affordance must remain legible while the player moves.
- Require state-linked character, potion, effect, and environmental motion. Ambient motion should support scene life without indiscriminate looping decoration.
- Whole-screen generations are composition and art-direction references only. Their component maps must separate environment layers, actors, props, effects, and UI into engine-native consumers.
- Individual assets must be derived from the approved target, preserve its palette/scale/material language, and be recaptured in Godot before approval.
- Reject generic purple cards, arbitrary rounded panels, excessive borders, mismatched pixel density, concept-art rendering, and procedural placeholders presented as final UI.
