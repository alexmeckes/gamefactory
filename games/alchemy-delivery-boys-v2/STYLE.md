# Alchemy Delivery Boys — visual starting constraints

The final direction is chosen from reviewed whole-screen gameplay targets after the graybox proves the loop.

- Aim for authored, expressive pixel art with strong character silhouettes and a warm, peculiar village identity.
- Use pixel art for the world and characters, but choose typography by information role; sustained reading must not be forced into tiny pixel text.
- The game viewport must read as a place characters inhabit, not a dashboard surrounding a picture.
- Potion state, hazards, interactable objects, destinations, and reactions need visual language that remains legible while moving.
- Ambient motion, interaction response, causal effects, and state transitions should make the scene feel alive without indiscriminate looping decoration.
- Generated full scenes are design targets, not runtime art. Individual assets must derive from an approved target, preserve scale and style, and be integrated and recaptured in Godot.
- Avoid generic purple cards, arbitrary rounded panels, excessive borders, mismatched pixel density, concept-art rendering, and procedural placeholder geometry presented as finished UI.
