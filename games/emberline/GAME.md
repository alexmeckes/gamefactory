# Emberline

## Hook

Defend a fragile glass heart on a dark workbench by placing three handcrafted
instruments beside one living fuse. Towers apply heat states rather than merely
stacking damage: Spark kindles, Bell cools and tempers, Striker shatters. Good
layouts compose those verbs into readable reactions.

## Product target

- A polished, mouse-first Godot 4 tower-defense vertical slice lasting roughly
  8–12 minutes.
- One authored serpentine route, deliberately chosen placement sockets, three
  mechanically distinct towers, at least four escalating waves, a boss, and a
  complete title → play → victory/defeat → replay loop.
- Decisions should include placement, upgrade/sell or rebuilding, timing a
  wave, and composing tower reactions. Avoid idle spectacle where the optimal
  action is obvious after setup.
- Teach through previews, silhouettes, projectile/state feedback, and compact
  language. Do not bury the first minute in a tutorial modal.
- The fantasy is a miniature workshop instrument, not medieval warfare.

## Open questions

The exact route topology, economy, reaction names, boss behavior, wave cadence,
upgrade structure, and degree of mid-wave intervention are open to candidates.
Agents should make a coherent hypothesis and test it rather than averaging
every possible tower-defense feature.

## Evidence boundary

Deterministic simulation can verify stability, towers firing, enemies moving,
reactions triggering, economy transitions, core damage, and wave completion.
It cannot establish that the strategy is satisfying or the presentation feels
premium; do not present proxy scores as human evidence of fun.
