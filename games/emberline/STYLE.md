# Emberline style direction

Emberline is a quiet, tactile workshop diorama: charred walnut, soot-black
iron, crazed bone ceramic, hammered brass, copper filament, wax, and a luminous
cyan glass heart. Warm ember light should feel precious against cool blue-black
shadow.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Route, enemies, tower reach/targeting, heat states, and danger must read in
   motion at normal gameplay scale.
2. The glass heart is the emotional focal point; incoming threats create a
   legible line of tension toward it.
3. Tower silhouettes must be recognizable without relying on color alone.
4. Material craft and environmental storytelling support the board instead of
   obscuring it.

## Tone

Handmade, intimate, ingenious, gently urgent, and slightly mysterious. Favor
physical cause and effect—shutters, ringing ceramic, coils, sparks, cooling
mist, recoil—over floating HUD magic.

Avoid generic green maps, medieval castles, neon sci-fi, mobile-game gradients,
glossy vector cards, excessive bloom, dense ornament, and tiny text. Respect
reduced-motion needs and never encode state by hue alone.

The pinned art target is aspirational evidence for material, lighting, and
composition. It is not a literal screen layout or permission to sacrifice
gameplay clarity.
