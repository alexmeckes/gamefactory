# Game contract

## Hypothesis

A single formation swap can produce a satisfying short autobattle when each slot changes both combat behavior and its visible choreography: the rear Seer chains power, the center Chime pulses healing, and the front Warden catches attacks on a shared ward.

## Scope

One encounter screen, three guardians, three enemies, one planning interaction, one automatic combat resolution, win/loss replay. No metagame, inventory, economy, progression, or content claims.

## Evidence boundary

Deterministic hooks establish that the intended formation reaches a resolved win and that health, attacks, healing, and battle state change. Engine stills establish rendering and interaction-state coverage; the engine-recorded motion clip establishes choreography coverage. None of these is human evidence that the game is fun.
