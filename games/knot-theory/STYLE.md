# Visual and audio direction

Aim for a tactile tabletop instrument rather than a generic neon tech demo.

- Dark slate or ink work surface with warm flax rope.
- Chalk, brass, coral, and bone-white accents, used by function.
- Soft rope shadow, clear tension highlights, restrained dust or fiber particles.
- Large readable shapes; no tiny nautical decoration competing with topology.
- Typography should feel like workshop labeling or a field manual.
- Sound should be sparse and physical: fiber creak, peg click, tension pluck,
  and a warm resolving chord. Silence is preferable to constant ambience.

The style is a constraint on coherence, not a template. Agents may reinterpret
the metaphor if their choice improves legibility and remains internally consistent.
